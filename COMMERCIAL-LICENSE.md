# Commercial licensing

Loomwerk is dual-licensed:

1. **AGPL-3.0-only** (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)) — free for everyone, including
   commercial use, as long as you comply with the AGPL. In short: if you run a
   modified Loomwerk as a network service or distribute it, you must make your
   modified source code available under the same license, and you must retain
   copyright and attribution notices (see [NOTICE](NOTICE)).

2. **Commercial license** — for organizations that want to embed Loomwerk in
   closed-source products, offer it as part of a proprietary SaaS, or otherwise
   cannot comply with the AGPL. Terms are negotiated individually.

For commercial licensing inquiries, contact **Thomas Winkler**
(via GitHub: [@GuideThomas](https://github.com/GuideThomas)).

## Why dual licensing?

The AGPL keeps the project honest: everyone who improves Loomwerk in a hosted
setting has to share those improvements. The commercial option keeps the
project sustainable without closing it. To make this possible, all external
contributions require a signed CLA (see [CLA.md](CLA.md)) so that the project
retains the right to offer both licenses.
